How to use

  1. Hold one ampule away from your face and crush it.

  4. Throw the used ampule away. One use only.

Storage

  • Keep unused ampules sealed in the jar until you need one.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from heat and direct sun.
  • Do not carry loose ampules where they can break.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
