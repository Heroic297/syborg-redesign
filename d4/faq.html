Product

Strong. The store describes them as made for hardcore athletes and not for beginners. If you are new to smelling salts, start farther from your face and take a short, light breath.

The bottle ($24.99) is pre-activated and reusable, so it suits daily training and your own gym bag. The ampules ($44.99 for 50) are sealed and single-use, so they suit meet day, sidelines, and teams where you don't want to pass one bottle around.

The bottle gives dozens of uses. How long that takes depends on how often you open it and how tightly you recap it. Ammonia escapes every time the cap is off, so open it only when you are about to use it and close it right away.

Uncap the bottle or crush one ampule at arm's length. Hold it about a hand's width from your nose, take one short inhale, then recap or discard. Never hold it against your nose.

Safety

Do not use them if you are under 18, or if you have asthma, any respiratory condition, or a head or neck injury. Never use them to wake someone up or to keep going after a hit to the head. If you have a medical condition, talk to a doctor before using any ammonia inhalant.

Avoid eyes and skin, use in a ventilated area, and stop if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
