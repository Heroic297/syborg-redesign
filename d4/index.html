How to use

Three moves. Keep it simple and keep it off your face.

  1. Uncap

    Open the bottle, or crush one ampule. Do it at arm's length, pointed away from your face.

  2. Hold

    Bring it up near your nose, still away from your face. Never press it against your skin.

    Holdaway from your face

  3. Breathe once

    One short inhale, then recap the bottle or toss the ampule. Step up and go.
