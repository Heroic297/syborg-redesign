FAQ

Answers drawn from the live product pages and labels.

What are Syborg Salts?

Syborg Salts are ammonia smelling salts made for hardcore athletes. They come as a multi-use bottle or as 50 single-use ampules.

Who are they for?

Made for athletes and favored by powerlifters, hockey players, football players, strongmen, weightlifters, and fighters. They are incredibly strong and not for beginners.

How do I use the bottle?

For first use, uncap, add a teaspoon of water, and shake. Hold the bottle at least 6 inches from your nose, take a quick sniff, then close the cap tightly.

How do I use the ampules?

Crush one ampule when you need it, hold it about 6 inches from your nose, inhale briefly, then dispose of it. Ampules are single-use only.

How far should I hold them from my face?

At least 6 inches from your nose. Never hold them directly under the nose or near your eyes.

How many uses are in the bottle?

The bottle is labeled for up to 250 uses. Actual uses vary with how you activate and store it.

Is there a subscribe option?

Where are they made?

The ampule jar is labeled Made in the USA.
