SYBORG SUPPORT

A little clarity.

Products, ordering, and the details that matter.

Which format should I choose?+

The bottle is resealable and designed for repeated use. Ampules are individually wrapped and single-use, so you can take only what you need. Compare the two formats below.

How do I use them?+

Read and follow the directions and warnings on your specific product label before use. The bottle is pre-activated; ampules require activation. Recap the bottle after use and dispose of used ampules.

Where do I place my order?+

Purchases are completed on the official Syborg store. Select “Shop the bottle” or “Shop the ampules” to open the corresponding product there. Final pricing and availability are confirmed by the store.

How much is shipping?+

Available shipping methods and costs are shown at checkout on the official store, before you pay.

What is the return policy?+

For current eligibility, time limits, and instructions, visit the Syborg refund policy ↗.
